Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Black Rim Solutions LLC ("Black Rim Solutions," "we," "us") handles information collected through this website and through the apps and services we publish (together, the "Services"). Individual apps may provide additional privacy details in the app or on their App Store listing; where they do, those details supplement this policy.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you email us or contact support, we receive your email address, name (if provided) and the contents of your message.

Information collected by our apps

Our apps collect only the information they need to work. Where an app requires an account, we store the details needed to provide that account, such as your email address and the content you create in the app. The App Store listing for each app describes the specific data it collects.

This website

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or third-party analytics. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Services
  • To respond to your questions and support requests
  • To keep the Services secure and prevent abuse
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the Services (for example, cloud hosting and, where an app offers it, sign-in with Apple, Google or GitHub). They process information only on our behalf and under their own privacy and security commitments.

Data retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing info@blackrimsolutions.com. Apps that let you create an account also let you request deletion of that account.

Children

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the date above.
