AI workflows

Put AI to work in your business

AI can take hours of repetitive work off your team's plate each week. I help businesses adopt it in practical ways: automating the busywork, connecting AI assistants to the tools you already use, and keeping a person in the loop wherever it matters.

Workflow automation

Hand off the repetitive work: intake forms, data entry, report generation, email triage and follow-ups. AI does the reading, sorting and first drafts, and your team does the deciding.

AI assistants on your data

Connect assistants like Claude and ChatGPT to your systems through secure integrations (Model Context Protocol and APIs), so your team can ask questions and get things done in plain language.

Document & data processing

Pull, summarize and organize information from invoices, contracts, emails and PDFs, turning stacks of documents into clean data you can search and use.

AI features in your apps

Add smart search, summaries, chat and recommendations to your iOS and web apps, using the right model for the job and keeping costs predictable.

AI-accelerated development

I build with modern AI-assisted engineering practices, so you get working software sooner and for less. Every change is still reviewed and tested by an experienced engineer.

Team training & adoption

Drawing on years as a bootcamp instructor, I run hands-on sessions that help your team use AI tools effectively and safely in their day-to-day work.
