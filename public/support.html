Support

Help with Black Rim Solutions apps and services.

Need help with an app or website from Black Rim Solutions LLC? We're happy to help.

Contact support

Email info@blackrimsolutions.com. We aim to respond within one business day.

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

  • The name of the app or service
  • Your device model and iOS version (for iPhone and iPad apps)
  • The app version (shown in the App Store or the app's settings)
  • A short description of the problem and the steps that led to it
  • A screenshot, if it helps

Common questions

How do I request a refund for an App Store purchase?

Purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. See Apple's guide for details.

How do I delete my data?

Email us from the address associated with your account and we'll delete your data. See our Privacy Policy for more.
